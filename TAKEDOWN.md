# Takedown Policy

ResPlan is released as a research dataset under CC BY 4.0. It contains only
derived, abstract geometric representations (polygons, room labels under a
17-class taxonomy, and connectivity graphs) of residential floor plans. No
source images, URLs, listing text, addresses, prices, agent identities, or
other listing-level metadata are redistributed.

## Reporting a concern

If you are a rights holder and you believe that a specific plan in this
dataset infringes your interests, please open an issue in this repository
titled `[Takedown] <short description>` and include:

1. A description of the rights you hold and the basis of your concern.
2. The plan identifier(s) (numeric ID as it appears in `split.json`) that
   you wish to have reviewed.
3. Contact information for follow-up.

Alternatively, you may email the dataset maintainers at the address listed
in the repository `README.md`.

## Our commitment

- We will acknowledge any good-faith takedown request within 7 days.
- Plans that are the subject of a substantiated request will be removed
  from the next dataset release within 30 days of acknowledgement.
- Removed plan identifiers will be tracked in `REMOVED.md` so that
  downstream users of prior dataset versions can update their splits.
- We do not redistribute source images and cannot provide them in response
  to such requests.

## Scope

This policy covers the data and annotations released in this repository.
It does not cover third-party reproductions, mirrors, or derivative works
maintained outside this repository.
